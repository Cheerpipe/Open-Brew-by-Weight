# Web UI screenshots

A tour of the Web UI as it looks on a phone, in light and dark mode, taken on
firmware 2.0.0 with a La Marzocco Linea Micra and a Bookoo scale. Personal
details, such as the account email, the machine's serial number, the network
name and local addresses, have been replaced with placeholders.

Long pages appear here one part at a time, the way you would read them: each
Settings, Diagnostics and Admin group is shown open on its own.

## Home

### The main screen

Home is where you brew from. The large card shows your last shot drawn against
the preset's cut line, with its weight, time and flow. Below it sit your
presets, ready to switch with a tap, and the state of your scale and machine.

### Header panel

Tap the scale or the shield in the header to choose how your next shots stop, by
weight or by hand, and to turn each guard on or off for now. Nothing here is
saved: picking another preset brings its own settings back.

## Shots

### Stats

Your averages and three charts that show how your recent shots landed: how many
were fast, on recipe or slow, how alike they are from one shot to the next, and
how close each stop came to the target. See [Your shots, in
numbers](../README.md#your-shots-in-numbers) for a friendly walk-through.

### Shot history
